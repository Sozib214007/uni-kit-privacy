About this policy

UniKit is a student toolkit application developed independently for university students, including students of Dhaka University of Engineering & Technology (DUET).

The app provides academic tools, cover page creation, CGPA calculation, task management, class routines, university information, student resources, community features and notifications.

Some features work locally on your device, while other features require an internet connection and use third-party services. These services may process information necessary to provide account access, verification, community features, notifications and advertisements.

In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" refer to the developer of UniKit. This policy explains the information we handle, why we use it, which service providers may process it, and how you can exercise your data rights.

Information we collect and handle

Depending on which features you use, UniKit may collect, receive, store or process the following categories of information.

Category Examples Purpose
Account information Google account email address, display name and authentication identifier. To sign you in, identify your account and provide account-based features.
DUET verification DUET university email address, verification status and verification-related records. To verify eligibility for features restricted to verified DUET students.
Profile information Information you provide or update in your UniKit profile. To maintain your profile and provide account-related functionality.
Community content Lost & Found posts, descriptions, submitted images where applicable, club update requests and academic calendar requests. To operate community features, process submissions and display relevant content.
Notification information Firebase Cloud Messaging device registration tokens and notification delivery or read-status information. To send relevant app notifications to your device.
Uploaded files Club logos, academic calendar images and other files submitted through supported features. To process submissions and display approved university-related content.
Advertising information Advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, ad interactions and information processed by advertising SDKs. To provide, measure and support advertisements.
Technical information Information that may be processed by service providers, such as app or device information, diagnostic information and service interaction data. To operate, maintain, secure and support the app and its third-party services.

The information processed depends on the features you use and the settings of your device and third-party services.

Information stored on your device

UniKit also stores certain information locally on your device to provide its features.

  • Cover page information: Names, student IDs, departments, course details, teacher details and other information you enter when creating documents.
  • Saved PDFs and History: Documents you create and save, including their titles, types and dates.
  • CGPA information: Semesters, subjects, credits, grades and previous CGPA values you enter.
  • Tasks: Task titles, types, course information, deadlines, notes and completion status.
  • Class routines: Class days, times, course names and room information you enter.
  • App preferences: Selected Quick Actions and other supported local preferences.
  • Guest session information: Information used to maintain your temporary guest session.

Local information is generally stored in the app's private storage. It is not automatically uploaded to our online services unless a particular feature requires you to submit or share that information.

Permissions

Internet access

Internet access is required for Google Sign-In, DUET verification, online university resources, community features, cloud services, push notifications and advertisements.

Notifications

UniKit may schedule local reminders for tasks and deadlines. It may also receive push notifications through Firebase Cloud Messaging. You can manage notification permissions through Android settings.

Photos and files

When you choose to upload an image or select a file, Android's system picker may provide access to the selected item. Uploaded files may be transferred to the cloud storage service used by that feature.

Advertising identifiers

Google AdMob may use advertising-related identifiers and device information to provide and measure advertisements, subject to applicable settings and consent requirements.

Device restart

If enabled by the app, Android may allow UniKit to restore scheduled local reminders after your device restarts.

Third-party services

UniKit uses selected third-party services to provide its features. These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Google Sign-In and Firebase

Google Sign-In is used for authentication. Firebase services may be used for authentication, database storage and push notifications. Account and app-related information may be processed through these services.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy

Google Apps Script

UniKit uses a Google Apps Script-based process to support DUET university email verification. Your university email address and verification information may be processed for sending and validating verification codes.

Supabase

Supabase is used for supported cloud storage features, including club logos and academic calendar images. Files submitted through these features may be stored and accessed according to the relevant app permissions.

Learn more: Supabase Privacy Policy

Google AdMob

UniKit uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob and its partners may process advertising identifiers, IP address, device information, ad interactions and other information necessary to provide and measure advertisements.

Learn more: Google Advertising Policies

Google Drive and external websites

Some academic resources may open through Google Drive, university websites or other external websites. When you use those services, their respective privacy policies and terms apply.

Advertising

UniKit displays advertisements using Google AdMob. Advertisements may appear in selected parts of the app, including banner and full-screen interstitial placements.

Google AdMob and its advertising partners may collect or process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, approximate location derived from IP address, and information about advertisement interactions.

Depending on applicable laws, your consent, device settings and advertising configuration, advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized.

You can manage certain advertising and privacy settings through your Android device and Google's available privacy controls.

UniKit does not sell your personal information. Advertising services operate under their own policies and may process information as independent service providers.

Sharing information

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with or processed by service providers when necessary to operate the app and provide its features.

Community features: Information you submit through features such as Lost & Found or other community features may be visible to other UniKit users where the feature is designed to display that content. Avoid submitting sensitive information that is not necessary for the feature.

Administrators: Authorized UniKit administrators may access relevant submissions, account-related records and moderation information to manage the app and review user requests.

Service providers: Google, Firebase, Supabase and advertising service providers may process information necessary to provide their services.

Sharing files: When you share or print a PDF, Android transfers the selected file to the app or service you choose. That service handles the file under its own privacy policy.

Legal and security requirements: Information may be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable laws, respond to lawful requests, protect users or protect the security of the app.

Storage and security

Local storage: Certain information, including created documents, saved PDFs, tasks, CGPA entries, class routines and preferences, is stored locally on your device.

Online storage: Account-related information, verification records, community submissions, notification records and uploaded files may be stored or processed using Firebase and Supabase services.

Security measures: We use security features provided by our service providers and take reasonable steps to protect information. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your responsibility: Keep your Google account secure and do not share your passwords or verification codes with anyone.

Data retention

We retain information for as long as necessary to provide the relevant app features, maintain account functionality, process user requests, maintain security and meet applicable legal or operational requirements.

Locally stored information generally remains on your device until you delete it, clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

Online information may remain in active databases or service-provider backups for a limited period, subject to the provider's retention and deletion processes.

Deleting your data

You can delete certain information directly inside UniKit. You may also contact us to request deletion of your account and associated personal information.

Delete locally stored information

  • Tasks: Delete individual tasks from the Tasks feature.
  • Class routine: Delete individual classes or clear the routine.
  • CGPA: Delete individual semesters or reset the calculator.
  • PDFs: Delete saved documents from History.

Request account and data deletion

If you have a UniKit account and want to request deletion of your account and associated personal information, contact us using the email below.

nextgenxstudio@gmail.com

Use the subject line: UniKit Account Deletion.

Include the email address associated with your UniKit account so that we can identify and verify your request. Do not send your password or verification code.

After verifying your request, we will take steps to delete your account and associated personal information from systems under our control, subject to applicable legal, security and legitimate retention requirements.

Some information may remain temporarily in service-provider backups or records where retention is necessary for security, fraud prevention or legal obligations.

Guest users

Guest session information is stored locally and can generally be removed by clearing UniKit's app data or uninstalling the app. Information separately submitted to online services may require a separate deletion request.

Delete files shared outside UniKit

PDFs or other files saved or sent to external apps and services are separate copies. You must delete those copies from the services or locations where they were saved or shared.

Turn off notifications

You can manage or disable UniKit notifications through your Android device's Settings.

Contact us

For data deletion requests or privacy questions, contact: nextgenxstudio@gmail.com .

Children

UniKit is designed for university students and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through UniKit, please contact us so that we can review the matter.

Not an official DUET app

UniKit is an independent student project. It is not an official application of Dhaka University of Engineering & Technology (DUET) and is not endorsed, sponsored or operated by the university.

The university's name is used only to identify the students and educational community for whom certain features are designed.
